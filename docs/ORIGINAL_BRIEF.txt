You are the lead game developer, technical director, gameplay programmer, level designer, environment artist, UI developer, build engineer, and deployment engineer for this project.

PROJECT NAME:
PIECE OF CAKE

CORE CONCEPT:
Build a polished third-person 3D action-platforming game called "Piece of Cake".

The game should feel like a premium stylized adventure platformer inspired by the gameplay principles of classic mascot platformers such as Crash Bandicoot, combined with the environmental adventure/exploration feeling of Tomb Raider and the visual wonder of cinematic fantasy games.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT copy Crash Bandicoot's characters, models, animations, environments, names, UI, sounds, music, branding, or exact mechanics.

Use only high-level genre inspiration:
- fast third-person platforming
- momentum-based movement
- jumping
- enemy encounters
- collectibles
- secrets
- environmental traversal
- cinematic set pieces
- readable linear level design
- optional exploration

The final game must have its own character, world, mechanics, visual identity, story, animations, UI, sounds, and level design.

==================================================
1. GAME EXPERIENCE
==================================================

The player controls a small, extremely cute but capable creature.

The character is visually small compared with the world.

The world should feel enormous.

The central contrast is:

CUTE CHARACTER
+
ANCIENT / MYSTERIOUS / BEAUTIFUL WORLD
+
FAST PLATFORMING
+
LIGHT DANGER
+
HUMOR

The game should NOT be horror.

It should be:
- mysterious
- adventurous
- beautiful
- occasionally dangerous
- playful
- cinematic
- charming

Avoid:
- gore
- disturbing horror
- jumpscares
- excessive darkness
- grimdark presentation
- overly childish environments

The character should be adorable while the world remains majestic and ancient.

==================================================
2. GAMEPLAY LENGTH
==================================================

The primary experience is ONE large continuous level.

Target first-playthrough gameplay time:

10–15 minutes.

The player should NOT feel like they are playing separate small levels.

It should feel like one continuous journey through one enormous interconnected environment.

The game should have a clear beginning, escalation, climax, and reward.

Structure:

START
↓
beautiful introductory area
↓
ruins
↓
forest
↓
temple
↓
underground/cavern section
↓
ancient city
↓
large cinematic traversal sequence
↓
final ascent
↓
cake reward

The transitions should be visually seamless whenever practical.

Use tunnels, caves, doors, traversal sequences, waterfalls, narrow paths, or other natural level-streaming boundaries when useful.

==================================================
3. THE STORY
==================================================

Keep the story simple and visual.

The character sees a piece of cake at the beginning of the journey.

The character wants the cake.

The cake becomes the player's simple motivation.

The joke:

The entire journey is extremely difficult.

The player survives:
- ruins
- enemies
- traps
- platforming
- environmental hazards
- collapsing structures
- caves
- ancient mechanisms
- large traversal sequences

The game repeatedly makes it feel like getting the cake should be easy.

It isn't.

The final reward is literally:

ONE NORMAL PIECE OF CAKE.

Do NOT make the cake a magical artifact.

Do NOT make it the source of world-ending power.

Do NOT overcomplicate the joke.

After the player completes the journey, the character reaches the cake, sits down or approaches it, and eats it.

The final title/reward moment should communicate:

"Piece of Cake"

The humor should come from the absurd contrast between the epic journey and the completely ordinary reward.

7. MOVEMENT SYSTEM
==================================================

Movement quality is one of the highest priorities.

Build a responsive third-person movement system.

Required:

- walk
- run
- sprint
- jump
- variable jump height
- air control
- landing detection
- directional air movement
- crouch/slide
- attack
- aerial attack
- ground slam
- bounce interaction
- ledge detection where appropriate
- basic climbing/traversal where appropriate

Movement should feel:
- responsive
- weighty but fast
- readable
- forgiving
- satisfying

Do NOT simply copy Crash movement values.

Develop an original movement model.

The player should be able to chain actions.

Example:

RUN
→ JUMP
→ AIR ATTACK
→ ENEMY BOUNCE
→ WALL/TRAVERSAL
→ LAND
→ SPRINT

The game should reward momentum and flow.

==================================================
8. PRIMARY ATTACK
==================================================

Do NOT use Crash's spin attack.

Create an original attack.

Recommended concept:

"BONK"

The character performs a quick physical forward strike.

Variants:
- ground bonk
- running bonk
- aerial bonk
- downward slam

The attack should feel playful rather than violent.

Enemies should react with exaggerated animation.

No gore.

==================================================
9. SIGNATURE MECHANIC
==================================================

Create an original environmental mechanic called:

ECHO

The character can activate certain ancient objects.

Echo temporarily reveals or reconstructs traces of the ancient world.

Examples:

A destroyed bridge temporarily reappears.

A broken mechanism becomes functional.

Ancient paths become visible.

Ghost-like environmental memories briefly show how an area was used.

Keep this mechanic visually spectacular but mechanically simple.

It should support:
- traversal
- environmental puzzles
- storytelling
- level progression

Do not turn the game into a puzzle game.

Echo interactions should generally take seconds, not minutes.

- ancient machinery

- subtle darkness

--------------------------------------------------

SECTION 6 — ANCIENT CITY
Duration: approximately 2 minutes

Use:
- huge towers
- bridges
- statues
- waterfalls
- mountains
- distant architecture
- atmospheric depth
- warm/cool lighting

--------------------------------------------------

SECTION 7 — COLLAPSE
Duration: approximately 1.5–2 minutes

Trigger a major cinematic traversal sequence.

The environment begins collapsing.

The player must:
- run
- jump
- slide
- attack
- bounce
- traverse
- avoid falling debris
- react quickly

Make this feel like a playable cinematic.

Do not take control away from the player unnecessarily.

--------------------------------------------------

SECTION 8 — FINAL ASCENT
Duration: approximately 1 minute

Everything becomes calmer.

The player climbs toward the final structure.

Reduce enemy density.

Increase atmosphere.

The player finally reaches the destination.
